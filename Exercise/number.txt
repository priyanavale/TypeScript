## Answer key

 Reveal answers with explanations (6–15) ## Quick revision: `number` in TypeScript

- `number` includes integers, decimals, `NaN`, `Infinity`, and `-Infinity`.
- TypeScript does not have separate built-in `int` and `float` types.
- `"25"` is a string, while `25` is a number.
- `NaN === NaN` is `false`.
- `toFixed()` returns a string.
- Decimal arithmetic can have floating-point precision errors.
