## Answer key

Try solving all 15 before opening this section.

 Reveal answers and explanations Remember these five rules for exams and interviews:

- Identifiers cannot start with digits.
- Letters, digits, `_`, and `$` are commonly used in identifiers; Unicode letters are also supported.
- Reserved keywords such as `class`, `return`, and `let` cannot be used as ordinary variable names.
- TypeScript is case-sensitive: `total`, `Total`, and `TOTAL` are different.
- Spaces and hyphens are not allowed in ordinary identifiers, but quoted object property names can contain them.
